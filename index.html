Best Diets for Sustainability

Multiple Approaches to Sustainability
There isn’t one single way to eat sustainably. Different approaches focus on reducing carbon footprints, supporting biodiversity, and contributing to the global economy.

Sustainability Doesn’t Mean Strict Veganism
  • You don’t have to follow a strictly vegetarian or vegan diet to eat sustainably.
  • A “conscious carnivore” approach allows you to include meat, dairy, and fish responsibly.
  • The focus is on choosing animal and plant foods that use fewer resources and generate lower greenhouse gas emissions

Key Differences Between Vegan and Sustainable Diets
  • Sustainable diets consider not just greenhouse gas emissions but also biodiversity and the nutritional value of foods.
  • While vegan/plant-based diets reduce emissions, they can be harder to maintain for many people and may pose a greater risk of nutrient deficiencies.
  • Sustainable eating aims for a balanced approach that is practical and nutritionally adequate for more people
The Goal of Sustainable Eating
  • Make mindful food choices that support personal health and the planet’s well-being.
  • Prioritize resource-efficient and environmentally friendly food options.
